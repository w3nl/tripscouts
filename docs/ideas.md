# Ideas for future regions

The grey dots on the map ("coming later"). When a region gets built, move its ideas into the code and AGENTS.md, and remove it from `SOON` in `js/regions.js`.

Every region follows the same recipe as the others (see AGENTS.md, "Regions" and "Region gameplay"):
- **eight days**, one level a day, with its own progress;
- a **fictional village or camping** where the families stay, at real places they visit;
- **its own gameplay**, not just another background: scenery (`scene`), ground (`theme`), creatures, decorations, and at least one thing that plays differently;
- every text in **English and Dutch**.

Småland (Sweden) is built now: see `js/region-smaland.js` and AGENTS.md.
Scotland is built now: see `js/region-scotland.js` and AGENTS.md.
The Ardèche is built now: see `js/region-ardeche.js` and AGENTS.md.
The Veneto is built now: see `js/region-veneto.js` and AGENTS.md.

## Transport for each trip

Some already exist; the others are ideas rather than plans or promises.

- **Ardennes:** a rail bike on the old railway: the bike stays on the rails while you jump over low ribbons and duck under branches. Built on day 2.
- **North Pole:** a reindeer sledge through the snow, jumping over ice blocks.
- **Tirol:** skiing and a toboggan run. Built on days 2 and 5.
- **Norway:** a kicksled on the way to the shop. Built on day 2.
- **Netherlands:** skating in Giethoorn and a white bike on the Hoge Veluwe. Built on days 5 and 6.
- **Dalmatia:** a sea kayak along the coast, with waves and rocks.
- **Småland:** a log raft on the lake, pushed with a pole, with floating logs to avoid.
- **Scotland:** a sailing boat on a loch, with gusts that change its speed.
- **Ardèche:** a canoe on the river, with rocks, waterfalls and rapids. Built on day 2.
- **Veneto:** a gondola through the canals, past mooring poles and low bridges.

## The other dots

Short notes to start from; add ideas here when they come up.

- **Iceland:** geysers, waterfalls, black beaches, puffins, northern lights, hot springs (warm water to swim in, in winter).
- **Tuscany:** cypress lanes, vineyards, hill towns, olive groves; summer or autumn.
- **Catalonia:** beaches, Barcelona's colourful buildings, mountain monasteries; summer.
- **Greece:** white houses with blue doors, islands, olive trees, ancient temples; summer.
